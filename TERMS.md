- Biblica is free. It is offered as it is, with no promise that it is free of mistakes.
- It is an aid to prayer and study. It is not an official book of the Church, and it does not replace the sacraments, your parish or a priest.
- What you write in it is yours and stays on your device. Keeping a backup is up to you.
- The code may be reused under the MIT licence. The texts and data that come from others keep their own terms, and a few short excerpts from the current Missal are not free to reuse at all.

## What Biblica is

Biblica is free software together with old texts that are in the public domain. There is no account, no payment and no service behind it: nobody is obliged to keep a copy online, to correct it or to answer questions about it. By using it you accept these terms. If you do not, please do not use it.

## Not an official book of the Church

Biblica has no imprimatur and has not been reviewed or approved by any bishop, by a bishops' conference or by the Holy See.

- The Bible is the Douay-Rheims in Bishop Challoner's revision. The Mass readings are shown in that translation, which is not the one read at Mass.
- The prayers are given in their long-established forms. The current English translations of the Roman Missal and of the Liturgy of the Hours are under copyright. The Liturgy of the Hours is not included. Of the Missal only short excerpts are given, in the Mass companion: the people's responses, the priest's short lines, and the first words of the longer prayers.
- The Mass companion is a guide to following the Mass. It is not the Missal, and it does not carry the full approved text of the ordinary form in English. Its English of the older Mass is a translation for following the Latin, not an approved text.
- The calendar, the holy days of obligation and the days of fasting and abstinence are worked out by the app for the United States or for the General Roman Calendar. They can be wrong, and dioceses differ. Your parish and your diocese are the authority.

## Not pastoral or medical advice

The examination of conscience, the guides to prayer, the notes on the saints and the commentary of the Fathers are aids. They are no substitute for a confessor, a spiritual director or the teaching of the Church, and nothing in the app is medical or psychological advice. If you are in distress or in danger, contact your local emergency number or someone you trust.

## Mistakes

The app contains mistakes. Texts were typed, matched and selected partly by machine; the historical notes were written for this project and only some have been checked against sources; verse numbers can be a verse off in a few books. The release notes (`RELEASES.md`) list what is known. Treat the app as a prompt to look something up, not as the last word.

## What you write

Your notes, journal and everything else you write belong to you and exist only on your device. They can be lost if the device is lost or reset, if the app is deleted, or if the browser clears its storage. Nobody can recover them for you. Make backups, and keep them somewhere safe: a backup file is plain text that anyone can read.
